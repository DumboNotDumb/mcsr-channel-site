Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy covers the "Daily Dose of MCSR Uploader" tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel Daily Dose of MCSR.

Who uses it

The tool is used only by the channel owner to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

Through YouTube API Services, with the owner's authorization, it accesses the owner's channel information, uploads, video metadata, and channel analytics (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube, yt-analytics.readonly).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and manage the owner's videos and to review channel performance. OAuth tokens and analytics snapshots are stored on a private machine controlled by the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Stored tokens are deleted on revocation or on request.
